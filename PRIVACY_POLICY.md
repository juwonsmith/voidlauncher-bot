# VoidLauncher Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 29, 2026

VoidLauncher is a Telegram bot that helps Telegram groups create, review, approve, and launch token proposals on supported launchpads such as Pump.fun and Bonk.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the bot collects, how it is used, and what controls users and group admins have.

## Information We Collect

When you use VoidLauncher, the bot may collect and store:

- Your Telegram user ID, username, first name, and basic Telegram profile details provided by Telegram.
- Telegram group IDs and group titles where the bot is added.
- Token proposal details submitted through the bot, including name, symbol, description, links, images, launchpad choice, and dev buy amount.
- Approval, rejection, and launch status for proposals.
- Wallet addresses created or used by the bot.
- Encrypted private keys for bot-created group or personal launch wallets, if wallet features are enabled.
- Transaction signatures, token contract addresses, launch metadata URLs, and related public blockchain data.
- Bot logs needed for debugging, abuse prevention, uptime, and launch troubleshooting.

VoidLauncher does not intentionally collect passwords, seed phrases, or unrelated private messages.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Run the bot inside Telegram groups and private chats.
- Save and manage token launch proposals.
- Allow group admins or authorized users to approve, reject, or launch proposals.
- Create and manage launch wallets when enabled.
- Submit launch requests to supported launch providers.
- Display token links, Solscan links, wallet balances, and launch status.
- Prevent unauthorized use of admin-only or wallet-export commands.
- Debug errors and improve bot reliability.

## Wallets And Private Keys

If VoidLauncher creates a wallet, the wallet private key is stored encrypted by the bot.

Private keys are not shown in group chats. Export commands are designed to work only in private Telegram DMs and only for authorized users.

Anyone who receives an exported private key controls that wallet and its funds. Keep exported keys secure and never share them publicly.

## Third-Party Services

VoidLauncher may send necessary data to third-party services to perform launches and related features, including:

- Telegram, for bot messages, commands, files, and group interactions.
- PumpPortal, for Pump.fun and Bonk launch requests.
- Pinata or other IPFS services, for token image and metadata uploads.
- Solana RPC providers such as Helius, for wallet balances and transaction checks.
- Blockchain explorers and trading tools such as Solscan, Axiom, GMGN, Trojan, or BasedBot, through public links generated by the bot.

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

## Public Blockchain Data

Token launches, wallet addresses, contract addresses, and transaction signatures are public on the Solana blockchain. Once submitted on-chain, this information cannot be deleted by VoidLauncher.

## Group Admin Access

If group-admin permissions are enabled, Telegram group admins may be able to approve, reject, or launch proposals from that group.

Only add VoidLauncher to groups where you trust the admins who may control launch actions.

## Data Retention

VoidLauncher stores proposal, wallet, and launch records for as long as needed to operate the bot, maintain launch history, debug issues, and support wallet recovery/export features.

Bot operators may delete old records when they are no longer needed, unless retention is required for security, troubleshooting, or operational reasons.

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect stored data, including encryption for stored wallet private keys when wallet features are enabled.

No system is completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting exported private keys, Telegram accounts, bot tokens, API keys, and admin access.

## User Controls

Users and group admins may:

- Stop using the bot at any time.
- Remove the bot from a Telegram group.
- Delete bot messages inside Telegram where Telegram permissions allow.
- Ask the bot operator to remove stored proposal or user records where practical.
- Export wallet keys only through authorized private DM flows, if enabled.

Public blockchain transactions and public IPFS metadata may not be removable after launch.

## Children

VoidLauncher is not intended for children. Users must follow Telegram's terms and any applicable laws in their jurisdiction.

## Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the bot changes. The latest version should be made available through the bot profile, website, or official project channel.
